# YesOS 8x16 bitmap font. Source of graphics/font_data.c (tools/gen_font.py).
#
# Each glyph: a line "U+XXXX <name>", then 16 rows of 8 pixels ('#' ink,
# '.' background), then a blank line. Metrics: capitals and digits on rows
# 2-10, lowercase x-height rows 4-10, ascenders from row 1, baseline row 10,
# descenders rows 11-13; rows 14-15 stay empty (line spacing).
#
# Origin: printable ASCII comes from the font written for the YesOS lab
# (yesos-lab graphics/font_data.c). Its shapes follow the look of the PC VGA
# font but it is not a copy of it: only 6 of its 94 glyphs match a VGA font
# byte for byte. Changed here (2026-10-15): g j p q y redrawn with real
# descenders (in the lab they stopped at the baseline) and ';' to match ',';
# added for Spanish and common text: á é í ó ú Á É Í Ó Ú ñ Ñ ü Ü ¿ ¡ ° « ».
# Part of the YesOS core, MIT licence.
